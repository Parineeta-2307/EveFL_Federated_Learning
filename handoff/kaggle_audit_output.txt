The audit passes, so you can merge the branch.
Why I'm confident

* No overlaps. All ten pairs show 0 patients and 0 images, and the script reports "disjoint at patient and image level".

   * Patients: 12,869 + 6,470 + 5,306 + 3,080 + 3,080 = 30,805, which matches the dataset.

* Realistic skew. The hospitals hold 42%, 16% and 22% of the images. Cardiomegaly has 1,868 positives in hospital 0 but 94 in hospital 1, and Hernia has 5 in hospital 0 but 86 in hospital 1. Test and validation each hold about 10% of the patients with a "No Finding" share close to the overall rate.

Two things to know

* Validation Hernia is thin. Validation has only 10 Hernia positives, so its per-class AUC is very noisy, and it feeds the macro AUC used for model selection. Decide the rule before any tuning runs, for example reporting macro AUC with and without Hernia on validation, or flagging classes with fewer than 20 positives. Test has 53 Hernia positives, which is acceptable for the final report.
* Hospital 0 barely sees Hernia (5 positives). That is the non-IID effect you wanted, but mention it in the paper so nobody mistakes it for a bug.
